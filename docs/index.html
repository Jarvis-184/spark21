Spark's tips basic strategy

Stand on 17 or more. Hit on 11 or less. With 12 to 16, stand if the dealer shows 2 to 6 and hit if they show 7 or higher. Double on 10 or 11 when the dealer shows a low card. A natural blackjack (an ace plus a ten-value card) pays 3:2.
